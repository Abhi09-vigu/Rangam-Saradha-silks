The Craft Behind Rangam Saradha Silks

“Every warp and weft is a sacred dialogue between human hands and pure mulberry silk — taking over 150 hours of undivided artisan dedication to produce a single heirloom drape.”

1
100% Pure Mulberry Silk

We hand-select premium Grade-A raw mulberry silk yarn. Double-twisted and rigorously inspected for high tensile strength, natural pearl-like luster, and lightweight drape.

2
Ancestral Pitloom Weaving

Master weavers maneuver pedals and shuttles on traditional wooden pitlooms. Each saree is created using generations of muscle memory and artisan wisdom with no automated machinery.

3
Authentic Zari & Motifs

Real electroplated silver and gold zari threads are meticulously woven into traditional royal motifs — Mayil (peacock), Annam (swan), Rudraksha, and majestic temple borders.

4
15 to 25 Days of Mastery

A single masterpiece takes up to 3 weeks of patient weaving. Once completed, every piece undergoes a rigorous 14-point quality inspection before reaching your wardrobe.
